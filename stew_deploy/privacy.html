S.T.E.W AI AGENT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Effective for all S.T.E.W surfaces (Telegram, WhatsApp, Web, API)

S.T.E.W ("Special Task Execution Worker", "we", "our", "the Service") is an AI agent product built and operated by MUTYINT. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use S.T.E.W across Telegram, WhatsApp, our web dashboard, and our public API, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account identifiers — your Telegram user ID, WhatsApp phone number (WA ID), or email address used to create a S.T.E.W account.
  • Messages you send us — text prompts, voice notes, photos, and documents you send to S.T.E.W for the purpose of generating a response.
  • Usage metadata — timestamps, feature used (chat, research, image generation, voice, video), and message counts, used for billing and rate-limiting.
  • Payment data — processed entirely by Paystack; we store only the transaction reference, plan purchased, and payment status. We never see or store your card number.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI responses to your requests (text, voice, image, video, document analysis).
  • To enforce free-tier limits and paid plan allowances.
  • To remember conversation context so S.T.E.W can hold a coherent, helpful conversation with you.
  • To improve reliability (e.g. debugging failed requests) — we do not use your content to train third-party foundation models beyond what each underlying AI provider's standard API terms already govern.

3. Third-Party Processors

S.T.E.W is built on top of infrastructure and AI providers that process data on our behalf under their own security terms:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Cloud API) — transports your WhatsApp messages to our servers.
  • Telegram — transports your Telegram messages to our servers.
  • AI inference providers (e.g. Groq, OpenRouter) — process your prompt text to generate a response. They do not receive your identity (name, phone number) — only the message content needed to respond.
  • Paystack — processes payments for subscriptions and S.T.E.W Coins.
  • Render — hosts our backend servers and database.

4. Data Retention

We retain conversation history to provide continuity of context. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 6). Payment records are retained as required by Nigerian financial recordkeeping obligations.

5. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to production data is restricted to the service operator. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or full deletion of your data at any time by messaging /deleteme to the S.T.E.W bot on Telegram or WhatsApp, or by emailing us at multipurposetalentedyounginven@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process deletion requests within 14 days.

7. Children's Privacy

S.T.E.W is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
